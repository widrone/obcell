01 / 05 · Executive overview

A TV studio with its own mobile network, in one small box

What it isOne small computer running a private 5G network and a complete live-production gallery: vision mixer, crew intercom and the tools to watch over it.

WhyTo broadcast from places with poor coverage, without an outside-broadcast truck or a phone company. Cameras and phones connect over our own radio.

How it is usedOne web address, one login. Mixer, intercom, monitoring and the on-air switch are browser tabs, reachable from anywhere. Guests only need a link.

Where it standsWorking on the bench, built entirely from open source. Going on air waits for SIM cards and a test permit.
